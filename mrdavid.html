Introducing Mr David AI

The most secure, autonomous, and intuitive AI voice assistant for Windows.

Built entirely by Ezeilo David Chisom. Mr David isn't just a chatbot—he is a fully integrated desktop companion that sees your screen, learns your habits, and controls your PC, all while locked behind military-grade voice biometrics.

Beyond the Browser.

Most AI assistants live in a web browser and can only answer questions. Mr David lives directly on your Windows desktop. He has native access to open your apps, type on your keyboard, scroll your web pages, and memorize your workflows without ever breaking your focus.

Engineered for absolute control.

🎙️ Total Voice Control

Trigger Mr David simply by saying his name. Tell him to open applications, search the web, type emails, or scroll pages—he physically executes system commands instantly on your machine.

🔒 Biometric Voice Lock

Your AI is fiercely loyal. Mr David uses advanced audio frequency analysis to recognize your unique voice print. If an unauthorized intruder tries to give him a command, he automatically locks down the system and refuses to comply.

🛡️ Privacy Guardian

Constantly watching your back. The built-in Privacy Guardian silently monitors background processes, outbound network ports, and clipboard modifications to detect and block malicious spyware or crypto-hijacking in real-time.

🚀 Predictive Autopilot

He learns your habits. The Autopilot engine tracks what apps you use throughout the day and automatically pre-launches your software workflows just before you need them, saving you hours of manual clicking.

🧠 Photographic Screen Memory

Mr David remembers everything you've seen. Ask him, "What was that phone number I saw on my screen 20 minutes ago?" and his photographic recall engine will instantly search his encrypted screenshot history to find the exact frame and answer you.

Built with extreme precision.

Proudly engineered from the ground up by Ezeilo David Chisom, showcasing complex, full-stack, AI-native desktop capabilities.

Frontend Architecture

Built with React 19, Vite, and Electron, featuring a translucent, glassmorphism UI overlay and dynamic WebSocket state management.

Backend Architecture

A robust, multi-threaded Python backend handling system-level commands, process monitoring (psutil), and local SQLite database encryption.

AI Brain

Powered by a redundant fallback mesh of state-of-the-art LLMs (Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Google Gemini 2.0 Flash, and Meta Llama 3 on Groq).

Speech Engine

Zero-latency audio transcription via Groq's Whisper API, paired with natural-sounding vocal generation via Microsoft Edge-TTS.

Distribution

Compiled into a single, fully-isolated 64-bit Self-Extracting Executable (SFX) that bypasses strict memory limits and natively installs local shortcuts.
